## t01-login
A mobile login screen: email and password fields, a 'Remember me' checkbox, a 'Forgot password?' link, and a primary Sign in button.

## t02-settings
A mobile settings screen with a top bar that has a back button. Show toggles for Notifications and Dark mode, an Account section with rows that have chevrons, and a Sign out button at the bottom.

## t03-music
Three phone screens for a music app: Home, Now Playing, and Library. Home and Library have a bottom tab bar with 4 tabs. Now Playing shows the queue in a bottom sheet.

## t04-dashboard
A desktop analytics dashboard: a 240px left sidebar with navigation, a header with a search field and the user's avatar, 4 stat cards in a row, and a table of recent orders with 5 columns.

## t05-product
A mobile product page: large product image, title, price, a size dropdown, a full-width Add to cart button, and a list of reviews.

## t06-checkout
A 3-step mobile checkout: Cart, Shipping details form, and a confirmation screen showing an 'Order placed' dialog.

## t07-tablet-mail
A tablet email client in split view: a list of emails on the left and the open message on the right.

## t08-files
A desktop file manager showing files in a table, with a details panel open as a drawer on the right.

## t09-onboarding
Three phone onboarding screens. Each has an illustration, a heading, a short description, and a Continue button, except the last, whose button says 'Get started'.

## t10-chat
A mobile chat conversation screen: top bar with the contact's name, the message thread, and a message input at the bottom with a send button.
