## k01-analytics
A desktop analytics dashboard: 4 stat cards in a row, a line chart of revenue over time, a bar chart of weekly signups, and a pie chart of traffic sources.

## k02-booking
A mobile hotel booking screen: check-in and check-out date fields, a guests selector, and a month calendar for October 2026 showing the selected stay from the 12th to the 18th. A primary Reserve button at the bottom.

## k03-verify
A mobile phone verification screen: a heading, 'Enter the 6-digit code we sent to (555) 010-2400', the code entry with the first 3 digits typed, a 'Resend code' link and a primary Verify button.

## k04-upload
A desktop upload screen: an onboarding stepper at the top on step 2 of 4, two files uploading at 40% and 85%, and storage used shown as a circular indicator at 72%.

## k05-pricing
A desktop pricing page with three plan cards (Free, Pro, Team), each with a price and a button. Each plan lists its features with check marks; features a plan doesn't include are shown with a cross and greyed out.

## k06-results
A mobile shop search results screen: a price range filter you can drag from both ends, a list of 5 products, and page navigation at the bottom showing page 2 of 9.

## k07-help
A help article page on desktop: a title, a short intro, the setup steps as a numbered list, and a bulleted list of tips.
